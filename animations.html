Animated Set: Robo Usher (Illustrator, After Effects)



Series of animations centered on a theme or item.
Focused on movement, contrast, textures, and character design during the project. We had to have an overarching theme or item that conected each of the animations. I began with the inital sketch of the Robot Usher and the concept of what kind of action could happen at a movie theater. The rest of the scenes came naturally after that with the usher's hand acting as a sort of character throughout each animation.

Created in Illustrator & AfterEffects (one of my first).
