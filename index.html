Hi, I'm Rachel. A detail-oriented designer, programmer, branding nerd, "master of whimsy", and lifelong learner.
Growing up as a military kid, I was always fascinated by the world around me and what I could learn by observing it. Using analysis and drawing from personal experiences, I'm able to create works that fuse structure with wonder, bringing together seemingly unrelated ideas to create something entirely new.

I like to experiment with different styles and mediums, always adding new skills to my toolbox. The more I learn, the less restricted I am at what I can create.
Connecting with clients personally is crucial for me to design projects that are meaningful and fundamentally human.


Why Chompy?
The name "Chompy" was originally inspired by my dog, a fun-loving Black Lab. It's also a great description of my work: bright, playful, and deeply personal.
