Fowl Heat (Card & Video Game, Unity — In Development)



Game developed in collaboration with NIMBioS at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville (UTK) under NSF grant 2411742.
Presented at Serious Play Conference 2025, 2026 Innovative Teaching & Learning Conference at UTK, and will be showcased at Meaningful Play 2026 in Pittsburgh.

In Fowl Heat, you play as an adult bird during the breeding season, working to maintain your own health and that of your chicks under intense, unrelenting heat.

Our small team of computer science students, graphic design students, and primary content authors created a game based on their behavioral heat research. As a student of both disciplines I worked closely between the two sides of the team, acting as a content translator when questions arose as a result of different working vocabularies. My primary work was creating assets for both digital and physical editions, developing rules that ensured cohesion between both editions, producing code and layouts for game UI/UX, organizing content for quick access by team, and conducting playtesting with primary content authors to ensure gameplay and rules were smooth and easy to understand.


Unlicensed Buyers (Card and Roblox Game)



Class project to develop a physical game. Team of 6 students from various fields of study.
In Unlicensed Buyers, it’s your job to dig through dirt to find diamonds. Choose a character and work quickly to find rare treasures and escape. Feeling stuck? Use your unique ability and shake up the game. Get your Grandma’s Old Recipes, invest in your retirement fund with unliquidated assets, or just straight up try and summon Cthulhu!


As Lead Designer and overall “platypus”, I worked closely with each team member to ensure the overall product was consistent. Primarily helped with mechanic/rule building, conducting playtesting alongside the Head Game Master, and creating the physical materials for initial playtests and the final physical game.

For the Roblox edition, I worked on everything from level and character design to code and everything in between. Heavily focused on user experience and trying to build something that was intuitive to play. I also took notes during every meeting to track what new ideas or changes were proposed to ensure the team stayed on track.

Santa Fe City Branding (Illustrator, After Effects, InDesign)



Class project to create a theoretical rebrand for the City of Santa Fe based on research of the city and its core values.
My initial research led me to focus on the natural landscape and unique landmarks. My goal was to evoke the historical, traditional, and vibrant atmosphere that earned the city its nickname, "The City Different". These were my guidelines as I created the brand set.

Deliverables included a website, guidebook, print and digital ads, and four aditional items of our choice.
I chose to create ID badges, a letter and envelope set utilizing the type system, a short animation of the wordmark, and a shirt with a woodblock print of the new logomark.


WRNO Radio (Radio Broadcasting)



"Tune in for your daily dose of the one and only Valentine Heart, Murano’s top model and designer!"

WRNO was a mix of branding and audio content for a limited-run radio station over the course of the Spring 2025 semester.
I used Audacity and GarageBand to create three one-hour sets that were played live at varying times. My focus was developing a unique sound profile and character who runs the station "late at night" in a futuristic, retro world. There were also various exercises in sound design that encourgaed us to sample sounds from our surroundings and find ways to create using the recorded ambience. Every project was experimental but gave students the chance to dive into sound design.

Each student was responsible for developing ther own radio station and broadcasts. For me this class was an exercise in worldbuilding and character development, immersing listeners in another place and time. The audio profile is fusturistically retro, while the storyline contains small details that add up to a much larger story.
